# 05 — Accessibility, WCAG 2.2 AA

Every item is a build requirement with a pass/fail check, not a suggestion. Findings are
closed in the build or waived here with a reason.

## Verdict contrast

- **Verdict text and glyph ≥ 4.5:1** against the card's own fill (1.4.3).
- **The card fill must be opaque.** A translucent card over a torch-lit camera feed has
  an indeterminate background and cannot be measured. Solid fill, no backdrop blur behind
  text.
- **Card border ≥ 3:1** against the video behind it (1.4.11). The DoorDash Dasher card
  fails this under a torch; ours must not.
- **Amber is the trap.** `#F59E0B` with white text is ~2:1 and fails outright. Ship amber
  as dark-on-light (≈`#78350F` on `#FEF3C7`, ~10:1) or light-on-dark (`#FFFBEB` on
  `#7C2D12`, ~9:1). Never mid-amber with white.
- **Verdict glyph ≥ 3:1** against the card (1.4.11) — the glyph is the fallback when text
  is unreadable through a cracked screen.
- Verify all three verdicts at **40% device brightness** and in **greyscale**. That is
  the real viewing condition, not a hypothetical.
- Honour `prefers-contrast: more` by raising to light-on-dark amber and adding a 2px card
  border on all verdicts.

## aria-live for verdicts

- **One persistent live region**, mounted once at app start. A region inserted into the
  DOM at the same tick as its content is frequently skipped by VoiceOver.
- Green → `role="status"` (polite). Red and amber → `role="alert"` (assertive), so the
  verdict interrupts.
- **Verdict word first**, one flat string: `"Admitted. Jamie Cole, GA. Checked in just
  now."` / `"Not checked in. No signal. Nothing changed, scan again."`
- **Clear then write.** Set the region to `''`, then write on the next frame — otherwise
  two identical consecutive verdicts announce only once, which is exactly the duplicate
  case where silence is dangerous.
- Visible content and announced string must match. No screen-reader-only phrasing that
  differs from the screen.
- Do not `aria-live` the camera preview, the frame, or the scan counter. A region that
  chatters gets ignored.

## Focus management

- **Auto-dismissing green does not move focus.** Moving focus on every success fights the
  scanner and the keyboard user. Green is announced through the live region only.
- **Red and amber take focus.** `role="dialog"` `aria-modal="true"`, focus to the card
  heading (`tabindex="-1"`), trapped, and **Escape** returns focus to the previously
  focused element.
- **2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured:** while a sheet or panel is up, no focusable control may
  sit underneath it. Move the torch / manual-code / list controls above the sheet, or mark
  them `inert`. A focused-but-hidden control is a fail.
- **2.4.13 Focus Appearance:** ≥2px solid outline, ≥2px offset, ≥3:1 against **both** the
  dark chrome and the live video. A default UA outline over a camera feed is invisible —
  draw our own with an outer contrasting ring.
- Camera panels and the gate are page-level content, not dialogs: move focus to the panel
  heading on mount, leave the rest of the page reachable.
- **2.1.2 No Keyboard Trap:** every trap exits on Escape *and* has a visible close.

## Target sizes

- **2.5.8** is 24×24 at AA. **We ship 44×44 minimum** — the AA floor is not a door
  standard, and one-handed use in the dark is the reason.
- Applies to: torch, zoom stops, manual-code button, List/Scan toggle, every filter chip,
  every per-row check-in control, every verdict button, every gate action.
- **≥8px clear spacing** between adjacent targets. The Apple Invites ✓/✕ pair at ~40px
  separation is the anti-pattern — an accidental reject at a door is not recoverable in
  the moment.
- **Primary per-guest actions in the bottom third** (thumb zone at 375×812 and at 320px).
  Undo and destructive actions go outside it.
- **Row targets are the full row height**, minimum 56px.

## Reduced motion

- **2.3.1 Three Flashes:** nothing may flash more than 3×/second. The duplicate-scan
  alert flash is what will violate this if written casually.
- **Preferred: no flash at all.** A single 150ms fill transition into the verdict colour,
  plus haptic (`navigator.vibrate` where available) plus a short audio cue. The colour
  *arriving* is the signal; repetition adds nothing when the staffer is already looking.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`: instant state swap. Keep the haptic, the audio
  and the text — **reduced motion must never mean reduced information**.
- No scanning-laser animation in the viewfinder, and no card entrance longer than 200ms.
  Entrance animations delay the verdict and count against S1.

## Never colour-only

**1.4.1.** Each verdict carries three independent signals, any two of which suffice alone:

| | Colour | Glyph | First word |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admitted | green | ✓ | **Admitted** |
| Rejected | red | ✕ | **Already scanned** / **Refunded** / **Cancelled** / **Wrong event** / **Not a ticket** |
| No verdict | amber | ! | **Not checked in** |

- Test by rendering in **greyscale** — every verdict must still be unambiguous. Not
  hypothetical; it is the cracked-screen, colour-shifted-display case.
- Guest-list rows follow the same rule: a checked-in row needs the word "In" plus a
  filled glyph, not a green pill alone.
- Filter chips: active state is not colour alone — `aria-pressed` plus a border-weight or
  fill change.

## Full keyboard operation (laptop door)

**2.1.1.** Every task completable without a pointer.

- **Tab order on the list:** search → filter chips → rows → per-row check-in. Chips are a
  **roving tabindex** group (one tab stop, arrows to move, Space/Enter to apply) so Tab
  does not walk through five chips to reach the list.
- **`/`** focuses search from anywhere on the list. **Escape** clears the query, then on a
  second press dismisses any open card.
- **Enter** on a focused row's control performs the check-in, announced through the same
  live region as a scan verdict.
- **Manual code entry** autofocuses its field and submits on Enter. This is the primary
  path at a laptop door with no camera.
- **Scan again** is keyboard-reachable inside the verdict card's focus trap.
- **2.5.7 Dragging Movements:** no drag-only interactions. Swipe-to-check-in needs a
  button equivalent; pull-to-refresh needs a Refresh control.
- **2.5.1 Pointer Gestures:** pinch-to-zoom needs the discrete zoom stops as an
  equivalent.

## Remaining AA items that bite this build

- **3.3.1 / 3.3.3:** the manual-code error is text, tied to the input by
  `aria-describedby`, naming a correction. A red border alone fails.
- **1.4.10 Reflow:** usable at 320px with no horizontal page scroll. The chip row scrolls
  horizontally; it must not force the page to.
- **1.4.4 Resize Text:** at 200% the verdict card must not clip or truncate the name. Cap
  the width, let it grow vertically.
- **1.3.1:** chips are a `role="group"` with an accessible name; the count is part of the
  accessible name, not a decorative span.
- **4.1.3 Status Messages:** the scan counter, offline bar and check-in-pending states are
  `role="status"` and must not steal focus.
- **3.2.6 Consistent Help:** the "Type the code" escape appears in the same position on
  every scanner state — scan, camera panel, verdict amber. Same label, same place.
- **1.4.12 Text Spacing:** no fixed-height verdict cards that clip when line-height is
  overridden.
